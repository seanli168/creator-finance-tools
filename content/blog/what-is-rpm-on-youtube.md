# What Is RPM On YouTube? Complete Guide To YouTube RPM In 2026

If you want to understand how much money a YouTube channel makes, one of the most important metrics to understand is:

**YouTube RPM.**

Many beginners look at views and subscribers, but experienced creators focus on RPM because it shows the actual revenue generated from content.

RPM helps answer questions like:

- How much money do 1,000 YouTube views make?
- Why do some channels earn more with fewer views?
- Which niches have higher earning potential?

---

# What Does RPM Mean On YouTube?

RPM stands for:

**Revenue Per Mille**

"Mille" means one thousand.

YouTube RPM represents the estimated revenue a creator earns for every 1,000 video views.

The formula is:
RPM = Total Revenue ÷ Total Views × 1,000

Example:

A channel earns:

Revenue:

$5,000

Views:

500,000

Calculation:
5,000 ÷ 500,000 × 1,000

RPM:

$10

This means the creator earns approximately $10 for every 1,000 views.

---

# RPM vs CPM: What Is The Difference?

Many creators confuse RPM and CPM.

They are related but different.

---

# What Is CPM?

CPM means:

**Cost Per Mille**

It represents how much advertisers pay for 1,000 ad impressions.

CPM is focused on advertiser spending.

---

# What Is RPM?

RPM is focused on creator earnings.

RPM includes:

- Ad revenue
- YouTube Premium revenue
- Membership revenue
- Other YouTube income

RPM is usually lower than CPM because YouTube takes a percentage of advertising revenue.

---

# Example:

Advertiser CPM:

$20

Creator RPM:

$8

The difference comes from:

- YouTube revenue share
- Non-monetized views
- Viewer behavior

---

Audience location has a major impact on RPM.

Higher RPM countries often include:

- United States
- Canada

- Lower advertising competition
- Lower purchasing power

# Why Is My YouTube RPM Low?

Many creators experience low RPM.

Common reasons:

---

## 1. Low Value Niche

Some topics naturally attract lower advertiser demand.

Examples:

- Memes
- Viral clips
- General entertainment

---

## 2. Audience Location

If most viewers come from lower advertising markets, RPM may decrease.

---

## 3. Short Videos

Short videos often provide fewer advertising opportunities.

Long-form content usually has higher RPM potential.

---

## 4. Weak Viewer Intent

Advertisers value viewers who are likely to purchase products.

Examples:

High value:

"Best accounting software for small business"

Lower value:

"Funny accounting videos"
