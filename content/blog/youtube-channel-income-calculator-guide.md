# How To Calculate YouTube Channel Income

The basic formula is:
Estimated Earnings = Monthly Views ÷ 1,000 × RPM

Example:

Monthly views:

500,000

RPM:

$8

Calculation:
500,000 ÷ 1,000 × 8 = $4,000

Estimated monthly earnings:

$4,000

---

# What Is YouTube RPM?

RPM means:

**Revenue Per Mille**

It represents how much a creator earns per 1,000 views after YouTube's revenue share.

RPM includes:

- Advertising revenue
- YouTube Premium revenue
- Other platform earnings

# Factors That Affect YouTube Income

A calculator provides an estimate, but several factors influence the final revenue.

---

# 1. Audience Location

Advertisers pay different rates depending on viewer location.

Higher-value audiences often include:

- United States
- Canada
- United Kingdom
- Australia

---

# 2. Content Niche

The topic of your channel strongly affects RPM.

Examples:

Finance:

Higher RPM

Gaming:

Usually lower RPM

Software:

Higher advertiser value

---

# 3. Video Length

Long-form videos often generate more revenue opportunities.

Long videos may include:

- Pre-roll ads
- Mid-roll ads
- More watch time

---

# 4. Viewer Engagement

Important signals include:

- Watch time
- Retention
- Click-through rate

Higher engagement can improve channel growth.

---
