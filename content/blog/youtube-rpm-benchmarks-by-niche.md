# YouTube RPM Benchmarks By Niche In 2026: Highest Paying Categories

Not all YouTube views are worth the same amount.

A channel with 100,000 views in one niche may earn significantly more than another channel with the same number of views.

The main reason is:

**Different YouTube niches have different RPM levels.**

RPM depends on:

- Advertiser demand
- Audience purchasing power
- Content topic
- Viewer location
- Commercial intent

Understanding RPM benchmarks can help creators choose more profitable niches.

---

# What Determines YouTube RPM By Niche?

YouTube RPM is influenced by several factors.

## 1. Advertiser Competition

Some industries spend more money on advertising.

Examples:

- Finance companies
- Software companies
- Business services

These advertisers often pay higher rates.

---

## 2. Audience Value

Advertisers care about the type of viewers watching videos.

A viewer searching for:

"Best accounting software"

is usually more valuable than someone watching:

"Funny videos."

---

## 3. Purchase Intent

Commercial audiences usually create higher RPM.

High-intent topics:

- Product reviews
- Software comparisons
- Financial advice

Lower-intent topics:

- Entertainment clips
- General viral content

# 1. Finance YouTube Channels

Finance is usually one of the highest RPM categories.

Examples:

- Investing
- Personal finance
- Credit cards
- Banking
- Insurance

Estimated RPM:

$15 - $40+

Why finance RPM is high:

- Financial advertisers pay more
- Viewers have strong purchase intent
- Products have high customer value

Finance channels can also earn from:

- Affiliate programs
- Sponsorships
- Financial tools

# 7. Gaming Channels

Gaming is one of the biggest YouTube categories.

However, RPM is usually lower.

Estimated RPM:

$1 - $5

- Large young audience
- Lower advertiser competition
- Lower purchase intent

Gaming creators often rely on:

- Sponsorships
- Memberships
- Merchandise
