# How Much Does YouTube Pay For 1 Million Views In 2026?

One of the most common questions from new creators is:

**How much money does YouTube pay for 1 million views?**

The answer depends on several factors:

- YouTube RPM
- Audience country
- Content niche
- Viewer engagement
- Monetization methods

A channel with 1 million views could earn anywhere from a few hundred dollars to tens of thousands of dollars.

---

# How Much Money Can You Make From 1 Million YouTube Views?

Most YouTube creators earn based on RPM.

RPM means:

**Revenue Per Mille (revenue per 1,000 views).**

The formula is:

For 1 million views:

| RPM | Estimated Earnings |
|---|---|
| $1 RPM | $1,000 |
| $5 RPM | $5,000 |
| $10 RPM | $10,000 |
| $20 RPM | $20,000 |

Your actual earnings depend on your channel.

# Why YouTube Earnings Differ So Much

Two creators can both receive 1 million views but earn very different amounts.

The biggest factors are:

---

# 1. Audience Location

Advertisers pay different prices depending on viewer location.

Higher RPM countries usually include:

- United States
- Canada
- United Kingdom
- Australia

Lower RPM markets often include regions with lower advertising demand.

Example:

A finance channel with US viewers may earn several times more than an entertainment channel with international viewers.

---

# 2. YouTube Channel Niche

The topic of your channel strongly affects earnings.

### Business
