# Why You Should Not Depend Only On YouTube Ads

YouTube ads can provide income, but they also have limitations.

Your ad revenue depends on:

- Number of views
- RPM
- Audience location
- Advertiser demand
- YouTube policies

---

## 1. Small Channels Often Earn Little From Ads

A channel with limited views may generate only a small amount of advertising revenue.

However, a smaller targeted audience can still produce significant income through:

- Affiliate products
- Services
- Digital products

---

## 2. Advertising Revenue Changes

YouTube RPM can change because of:

- Seasonality
- Market conditions
- Advertiser budgets
- Content category

Multiple income streams provide more stability.

---

## 3. Creators Have Limited Control

Creators do not control:

- Algorithm changes
- Recommendation systems
- Advertising rates

Building your own business assets reduces platform dependency.
