# Best Affiliate Programs For YouTubers In 2026: How To Earn More From Videos

Many YouTubers focus only on advertising revenue.

However, ads are not the only way to make money from a YouTube channel.

Affiliate marketing has become one of the most profitable income streams for creators.

With affiliate programs, YouTubers can earn money by recommending products they already use.

Popular affiliate categories include:

- AI tools
- Video editing software
- Creator equipment
- Website tools
- Marketing platforms
- Online courses

The biggest advantage:

You do not need millions of views.

A small YouTube channel with the right audience can generate significant affiliate income.

---

# What Is YouTube Affiliate Marketing?

YouTube affiliate marketing is when creators earn commissions by promoting products through affiliate links.

The process:

1. Join an affiliate program
2. Receive a unique tracking link
3. Add the link to videos
4. Viewers click and purchase
5. Earn commissions

Example:

A creator publishes:

"Best AI Video Editing Tools For YouTubers"

The description includes affiliate links.

A viewer purchases an AI tool.

The creator earns a commission.
