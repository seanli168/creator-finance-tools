# How Do YouTube Shorts Creators Make Money?

YouTube Shorts creators can earn money through several methods:

Revenue is pooled and distributed based on creator performance.

# YouTube Shorts RPM Explained

Shorts RPM means:

**Revenue earned per 1,000 Shorts views.**

Shorts RPM is usually much lower than long-form RPM.

Typical Shorts RPM:
$0.01 - $0.10+


Long-form YouTube videos may achieve:
$1 - $30+


depending on niche.

---

# Why Is YouTube Shorts RPM Lower?

There are several reasons.

---

## 1. Lower Ad Placement

Long videos can include:

- Pre-roll ads
- Mid-roll ads
- Display ads

Shorts mainly rely on feed advertising.

---

## 2. Revenue Sharing Model

Shorts revenue is distributed through a shared pool.

This differs from traditional video advertising.

---

## 3. Viewer Behavior

Shorts viewers often:

- Watch quickly
- Scroll frequently
- Have lower purchase intent
