# How YouTubers Make Money With Affiliate Links In 2026

Many people think YouTubers only make money from ads.

However, successful creators often use multiple income streams.

One of the most powerful methods is:

**Affiliate marketing.**

YouTubers can earn money by recommending products and earning commissions from viewers who purchase through their links.

Affiliate income allows creators to monetize their audience even when:

- Their channel is small
- Their videos receive fewer views
- They are not eligible for YouTube ads

---

# What Are YouTube Affiliate Links?

A YouTube affiliate link is a special tracking URL that identifies customers referred by a creator.

The process:

1. Creator joins an affiliate program
2. Company provides a unique link
3. Creator shares the link in videos or descriptions
4. Viewer clicks and purchases
5. Creator earns a commission

---

# How Do YouTubers Make Money With Affiliate Links?

The basic formula is:
Traffic × Click Rate × Conversion Rate × Commission = Affiliate Income

# How To Increase YouTube Affiliate Income

## 1. Create Buyer-Focused Content

The best affiliate videos target people looking for solutions.

Examples:

Good:

"Best AI Video Tools Compared"

Weak:

"My Thoughts About AI"

---

## 2. Choose The Right Products

The highest commission product is not always the best.

Choose products with:

- Good reputation
- Strong demand
- Real value

---

## 3. Explain Benefits Clearly

Do not simply say:

"Buy this tool."

Explain:

- Why you use it
- What problem it solves
- Who should use it

---

## 4. Create Comparison Content

Comparison videos perform well.

Examples:

- Product A vs Product B
- Free vs Paid
- Beginner vs Professional

---

## 5. Build A Content Funnel

Example:

Short video:

"3 AI Tools You Need"

↓

Long video:

"Complete AI Workflow Guide"

↓

Affiliate links

↓

Recurring commissions

# How Many Subscribers Do You Need For Affiliate Marketing?

There is no minimum requirement.

A small channel can succeed if:

- Audience is targeted
- Content solves problems
- Products match viewers

A 5,000 subscriber software channel may outperform a 100,000 subscriber entertainment channel.
