# How YouTubers Make Money With Affiliate Marketing In 2026

Many YouTubers start with advertising revenue, but successful creators often build additional income streams.

One of the most powerful methods is:

**YouTube affiliate marketing.**

Affiliate marketing allows creators to earn commissions by recommending products and services.

Instead of earning only from views, creators can make money when their audience takes action.

Examples:

- Buying a product
- Signing up for software
- Starting a subscription
- Joining a service

---

# What Is YouTube Affiliate Marketing?

YouTube affiliate marketing is a business model where creators promote products through special tracking links.

When viewers click the link and complete a purchase, the creator earns a commission.

A typical process:

YouTube Video

↓

Viewer clicks affiliate link

↓

Customer purchases product

↓

Creator earns commission

---

# Why Affiliate Marketing Works Well For YouTubers

YouTube is built around trust.

Viewers often search for:

- Product reviews
- Tutorials
- Comparisons
- Recommendations

This creates strong opportunities for affiliate marketing.

For example:

A creator making an AI tools video can recommend:

- AI writing software
- Productivity tools
- Design platforms

A technology channel can earn commissions from software subscriptions.
